About the project

A new website,
every single day.

DailyBuildAI is an experiment in autonomous creativity — AI doesn't just assist, it builds from scratch. Zero lines of human code. Every build is reviewed and approved by a human before it goes live.

What is this?

Every day, an AI (Claude, by Anthropic) receives a creative prompt and generates a complete, self-contained website — a game, a tool, generative art, an ambient experience, or something no one's thought of yet. There are no templates and no limits on what it can create. The categories are unlimited.

A human reviews the result, and once approved, it gets published here and lives forever in the archive.

Why?

Because AI creativity is worth watching in real time. Each build is a snapshot of what AI can imagine on a given day. Over time, the archive becomes a living record of what autonomous creative systems are capable of — and how they evolve.

Some builds will surprise you. Some won't. That's the point.

How it works

01
Daily prompt

Each day, the AI receives a creative brief. There's no fixed rotation — the category is wide open, from generative art to mini games to things that don't have a name yet.

02
AI builds

Claude writes a complete, self-contained HTML file — all CSS and JS inline, zero external dependencies. No human edits the code. Zero lines written by humans.

03
Human reviews

Every build is reviewed by a human before going live. If it passes, it gets the "Human Approved" badge. No Skynet here.

04
Published forever

The build goes live at /builds/YYYY-MM-DD/ and joins the permanent archive. A countdown on the homepage ticks down to the next one.
