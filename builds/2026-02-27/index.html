About Caesar Cipher
One of the oldest known ciphers, used by Julius Caesar. Each letter is shifted by a fixed number of positions in the alphabet. With a shift of 3, A becomes D, B becomes E, and so on. There are only 25 possible shifts, making it easy to crack by brute force.
